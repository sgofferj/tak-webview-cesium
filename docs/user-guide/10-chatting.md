# Chapter 10 — Chatting

The chat connects you with everyone else on your TAK network — both this
web map and regular ATAK devices. Messages are plain text; there are group
rooms and one-to-one direct messages.

## Opening chat

Click the **chat toggle** in the status bar. A panel slides out:

- **Left: channel list** — Rooms at the top, Users below.
- **Right: conversation view** with the message input at the bottom.

A red badge on the chat toggle counts your unread messages across all
conversations.

## Rooms

Rooms are group conversations. Several are created for you automatically:

- **All Chat Rooms** — everyone on the network. The default room.
- **One room per team color** — only members of that color (for example
  everyone in Red). Shown as a colored circle.
- **One room per role** — for example all Medics or all Team Leads.
  Team Members do not get a separate role room. Shown as a white circle
  with the role abbreviation.

Rooms appear as soon as a member of that color or role is visible to you,
so the list grows and shrinks with who is actually online.

## Direct messages

Under **Users**, every currently visible person is listed with their map
icon. Click a name to open a private conversation with them — even if you
have never exchanged messages before.

Direct messages are delivered **only to that person**, not to the room.

## Sending messages

1. Select a room or user.
2. Click into the message field (or just start typing).
3. Press **Enter** or click **Send**. `Shift+Enter` inserts a new line.

Your message appears immediately, slightly faded until confirmed, then
solid once it is on the network.

## Delivery and read receipts

After each message you sent, small checkmarks tell you its status:

| Display | Meaning |
| ------- | ------- |
| *(nothing)* | Sent — on its way |
| **✓** | Delivered — reached the recipient's device |
| **✓✓** | Read — the recipient has viewed it |

Likewise, reading a conversation automatically confirms its messages to
the sender.

## Unread badges

Each channel in the list shows how many unread messages it holds (up to
99+). Opening the channel clears its badge. Messages received while the
chat panel is closed still accumulate badges, so you can keep the panel
shut while monitoring.

## Message history

When you sign in, recent history of each conversation is shown. History is
kept in memory on the server side for a limited amount of time and depth
— it is not an archive. For permanent records, use your unit's reporting
procedures.
