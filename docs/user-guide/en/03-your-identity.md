# Chapter 3 — Your Identity: Callsign, Color and Role

## Why this matters

Everything you do on the network — your map symbol, your chat messages,
the rooms you appear in — is tied to the identity you set here. Other
users see this identity, so choose it in line with your unit's naming
conventions.

The setup window appears automatically after your first connection. You can
reopen it any time with the **config button** (gear/settings) in the status
bar at the bottom of the screen.

The first time, it may already be partly filled in — some TAK servers push
a profile with your assigned callsign and team. If so, just check the
values and confirm.

## The three settings

### Callsign *(required)*

- This is your name on the network, for example `BRAVO-1` or
  `MED-EVAC-2`.
- It must be filled in before you can save or close the window.
- Keep it short and recognizable; everyone in chat will see it.

### Team color

- Selects your team, shown as a colored circle around your position on
  other users' maps: White, Yellow, Orange, Magenta, Red, Maroon, Cyan,
  Dark Cyan, Blue, Dark Blue, Green, Dark Green, Brown or Purple.
- Users of the same color are grouped into a shared chat room
  automatically (Chapter 10).

### Role

- Describes your function on the team: Team Member, Team Lead, HQ,
  Sniper, Medic, Forward Observer, RTO, K9 or Pilot.
- The role appears abbreviated next to your callsign (for example
  **TL** for Team Lead, **MD** for Medic).

## Saving

1. Enter your callsign.
2. Pick your team color and role.
3. Click **Save**.

Saving also starts your live connection — from this moment you are visible
to other users and they are visible to you.

> **Note:** Changing your identity later takes effect immediately, but the
> connection restarts briefly while the change is announced to the network.

## Your location

Your symbol is placed where *you* say you are. To put yourself on the map
at your actual position, see Chapter 9.
