# Chapter 7 — Finding Units

## The unit list (Active Tracks)

The **Active Tracks** section of the sidebar lists everything currently
visible on the map, organized for quick scanning:

- Top level: categories — Incidents, Aircraft, Vessels and Other.
- Within each category: grouped by affiliation (Friendly, Hostile,
  Suspect, Neutral, Unknown).
- Each entry shows the unit's icon and callsign in its display color;
  active emergencies get a red badge.

Click a category header to fold or unfold it. Click a unit to fly the map
to it.

## Searching with text

The search box at the top of the sidebar filters everything live as you
type. It matches against:

- Callsigns
- Unit IDs
- Remarks text (including hashtags)

Example: typing `bravo` keeps only units whose name or remarks contain
"bravo". Clear the box (or press the **Clear** button) to show everything.

## Filtering by affiliation

The **Affiliation** dropdown lets you pick one or more sides — for example
show only Friendly and Neutral traffic, or only Hostile. Use the *select
all* option to re-enable everything quickly.

## Filtering by dimension

The **Dimension** dropdown filters by battlespace: Air, Ground, Surface,
Subsurface, Space, Special Forces or Other. The options are built from
what is actually on the map right now — if no aircraft are connected, Air
does not appear.

Filters are remembered: they survive closing the browser and are restored
when you return.

## Hashtags

Remarks often contain hashtags (`#patrol-north`, `#sf`). In any unit's
info box these render as clickable links; clicking one applies it as the
text filter, instantly showing only units that carry the same tag. This is
the fastest way to isolate a group of interest.

## Staff Comments panel

The **Staff Comments** section groups units by keywords your administrator
defined (for example `#SF` = Shadow Fleet). Each keyword becomes its own
collapsible group listing matching units. If nothing appears there, either
no unit currently matches or none were configured — the panel stays empty
rather than showing errors.

## What "visible" means

All lists and filters operate on what is on your map at this moment. Units
that stopped reporting (stale) disappear automatically after their data
expires, so the list always reflects the current picture.
