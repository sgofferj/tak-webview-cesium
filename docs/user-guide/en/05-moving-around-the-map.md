# Chapter 5 — Moving Around the Map

## Basic movements (mouse)

| Action | How |
| ------ | --- |
| Move the map | Click and **drag** with the left mouse button |
| Zoom in / out | Roll the mouse wheel, or double-click to zoom in |
| Tilt to a 3D view | Hold the **right** mouse button (or `Ctrl` + drag) and drag up/down |
| Rotate the view | Hold the **right** mouse button and drag left/right |

On a touchscreen: one finger pans, pinch zooms, two fingers rotate and
tilt.

## Reset View

The **Reset View** button snaps the view back to looking straight down,
with north up, from a sensible height. Use it whenever you feel "lost" —
it always brings you back to a clean overhead orientation.

## Zoom to All

**Zoom to All** fits every currently visible unit into your view at once.
It ignores extreme outliers (for example a single unit on another
continent) so your map does not shrink to a globe view.

## GoTo buttons

If your administrator has configured them, quick-jump buttons appear
labeled with places or objectives (for example `CP`, `HARBOR`, `RANGE
ALPHA`). One click flies the camera there.

## Watching a unit

Click a unit symbol to select it; its info box opens (Chapter 6) and its
recent movement trail becomes visible. The selection also follows moving
symbols: clicking a unit's trail or course arrow selects the unit itself.

## Camera angle indicator

The **Reset View** button doubles as an indicator: while your view is
tilted or rotated it shows one icon; when you are straight down and north
up it shows another. If you are unsure how your camera is oriented, glance
at that button.
