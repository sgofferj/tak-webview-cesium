# Chapter 1 — Getting Started

## What you need

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. **The web address of your unit's map viewer.** Your administrator gives
   you this. It looks like a normal website address and opens in your
   browser.
2. **A user account on your unit's TAK server** (username and password), or
   **a personal certificate file** (a `.p12` or `.pfx` file) with its
   password. Which one you use depends on how your unit set things up —
   see Chapter 2.
3. **A computer or tablet** with a current web browser (Firefox, Chrome,
   or Edge). No installation is needed.

## Opening the application

1. Open your browser.
2. Enter the address your administrator gave you.
3. If your organization uses a security warning page or a login page before
   the map loads, sign in there as usual.

The first time you connect, you will see a welcome screen asking **how you
want to connect** to the network. Chapter 2 explains that screen.

> **Tip:** Bookmark the address so you can find it quickly next time.

## What happens when you connect

Once you are signed in, your browser connects to your unit's TAK server.
From that moment:

- You appear on everyone else's map as a unit symbol, using the callsign
  and team color you choose in Chapter 3.
- Their positions appear on your map.
- Chat rooms and messages become available to you.

Your connection is only active while the map page is open. If you close the
browser tab, you disappear from other users' maps after a short while.

## Languages

The application follows the language setting of your browser. It supports
English, German, Swedish and Finnish. To change the language, change your
browser's display language and reload the page.
