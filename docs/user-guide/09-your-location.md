# Chapter 9 — Setting Your Own Location

Your map symbol is placed where you tell the system you are. If you do not
set a location, your symbol sits at your account's default position (or at
the map origin) until you do.

## Setting your position

1. Click **Location** in the status bar.
2. A small menu appears with two choices:

### Option A — Pick on the map

- Click **Use map**.
- The button changes to *Cancel Location* to show that picking is active.
- Click anywhere on the map — ideally zoomed in to your actual position
  first, so the click is accurate.

### Option B — Use the device's position

- Click **Use browser location**.
- Your browser asks for permission to share your position; allow it.
- The device determines its position (most accurate on devices with GPS).

## After setting it

- A **blue triangle marker** appears at your position on the map.
- Your coordinates are shown next to the **Location** button, so you can
  read them off at any time.
- Other users see your symbol move to the new position within moments.

The position is remembered on this device and re-applied automatically the
next time you sign in.

## Changing or correcting it

Just set it again — pick on the map for precision, or use browser location
after moving.

## Privacy note

Your position is shared with everyone on your TAK network — that is its
purpose. Set it deliberately: if you are working from an office while your
callsign represents a team in the field, place your symbol where the team
is, not where your computer is.
